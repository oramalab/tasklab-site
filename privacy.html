Privacy Policy

TaskLab for Mac and iPhone · Last updated October 18, 2026

TaskLab is built so that your tasks stay yours. We don't collect, see, sell or share any of your data.

What TaskLab stores

Your tasks, lists, tags, notes and attached files are stored on your devices. If you're signed in to iCloud, TaskLab syncs them through your own private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit). That database belongs to your Apple Account; we, the developer, have no access to it. Apple's handling of iCloud data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

What we collect

Nothing. TaskLab has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party code that collects data.

Notifications

If you set a due time, TaskLab schedules a local notification on your device. Notifications are created on the device and never pass through our servers (we don't have any).

Deleting your data

Delete tasks in the app (they're removed permanently from Trash after 30 days, or right away with Empty Trash). Deleting the app removes its local data; to remove synced data, delete TaskLab's data in Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage.

Children

TaskLab doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
