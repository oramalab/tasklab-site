Quick add

  • Dates and times: “tomorrow 3pm”, “next fri”, “Oct 24”
  • Priority: !high, !med, !low
  • Tags: #tag (created if new)
  • List: ^list (matches the start of a list name)

Syncing

TaskLab syncs through your private iCloud account. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account on each device and that iCloud Drive is turned on. The sync status is shown at the bottom of the sidebar on Mac; details are in Settings → Sync.

Files in notes

Drag images, documents or videos into a task's notes, paste them, or use the paperclip. Remove one like text: put the cursor after it and press Delete. Removed files can be restored with Undo for 30 days.

Reordering

Press and hold a task, move it, and let go where you want it.

Keyboard shortcuts (Mac)

  • ⌘N new task · ⇧⌘N new list · ⌘F search
  • ⌘↩ complete · ⌘⌫ move to Trash · ⌘D duplicate
  • ⌥⌘T due today · ⌥⌘Y due tomorrow · ⌥⌘1–3 priority
  • ⌘= / ⌘− text size · ⌘0 actual size
